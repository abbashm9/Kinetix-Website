## The shift
v1 listed what we operate (reporting, inbox assistant, websites, content, ads, brand).
v2 sells what the owner ends up with. Tools move behind the promise.

Four outcomes, in the order a business owner feels the pain:

| # | Outcome (owner's words) | What we run to deliver it | How the owner sees it worked (own data, no promised numbers) | Proof available |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "People find us and trust us." | Brand identity, website, search and AI-answer visibility (llms.txt, cost articles) | Site live, brand applied everywhere, shows up when asked in ChatGPT/Google | Al Jadad, Samah, Bonyad |
| 2 | "Our name is in front of customers every week." | Content Engine, Meta ads management | Posts shipped on schedule, ad spend and enquiries side by side | Samah, Bonyad |
| 3 | "Every enquiry gets an answer, even when I'm busy." | AI Inbox Assistant (pilot label stays) | Enquiries answered and passed to WhatsApp or email | Pilot only, task 8 blocked |
| 4 | "I know what worked without chasing anyone." | AI-powered monthly reporting | One plain report each month, in the owner's language | Kinetix reports |

## Recommended packaging (decision for Abbas)
Sell ONE headline outcome on the homepage: **"Enquiries you can see."** Outcomes 1 and 2 are how we get them, 3 keeps them, 4 proves them.
Homepage acts become: found -> chosen -> answered -> proven -> one conversation. Services pages stay as the detail layer.
