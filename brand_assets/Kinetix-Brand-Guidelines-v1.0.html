02.5

Logo Construction

The Kinetix mark is engineered on a geometric grid. Every measurement derives from the height of the "K" lettermark — our master unit, referred to as 1X. Understanding this system prevents visual drift and keeps the mark recognizable at every scale.

The Two-Mark System
Primary Mark

The Kinetix Monogram

The signature K mark — a bold vertical stem with an angular split-leg construction implying forward motion. Used wherever the brand needs full presence.

USE FOR → Letterheads · Website · Decks · Print · Signage
App & Favicon Mark

The Gradient Tile

The same K monogram set inside a rounded gradient tile. Used only where scale or rendering constraints demand a bolder, high-contrast form.

USE FOR → App icons · Favicons · Social avatars · Stickers
⚠ Do not interchange. The monogram and the gradient tile are not substitutes for each other. Never use the gradient tile on a letterhead; never use the monogram as an app icon at 48px. Each mark is optimized for its context.

04.5

Voice & Tone

A marketing agency's real deliverable is language. This section defines how Kinetix sounds — whether in a cold email, a social caption, a proposal, or a boardroom. If our visuals set us in motion, our words tell the world where we're going.

We write like we design:
fast, precise, and impossible to ignore.
— Kinetix Voice Principle
Voice Attributes — The Four Dials
01
Confident
Not arrogant
We make claims we can defend. We speak in active voice. We don't hedge with "maybe" or "perhaps" when we mean "yes."
02
Sharp
Not cold
Short sentences. Clear verbs. We cut filler. Every word earns its place. But sharp isn't robotic — there's rhythm underneath.
03
Human
Not casual
Contractions welcome. Jokes allowed. We sound like a person, not a press release — while still sounding like a professional one.
04
Grounded
Not boastful
Numbers over adjectives. Proof over promise. Instead of "award-winning creative" say "47% lift in 90 days."

Usage Rules

Consistent logo usage protects brand equity. These rules are mandatory across all communications.

✓ Always Do
✓Use the approved logo files only — never recreate the wordmark manually
✓Maintain the minimum clear space on all sides of the logo
✓Use the correct color variant for the background (dark logo on light, light logo on dark)
✓Use only the approved typefaces: Outfit (EN) and Cairo (AR)
✓Use the gradient version sparingly — for hero moments and key CTAs only
✓Ensure sufficient contrast — minimum WCAG AA (4.5:1) for text
✓When Arabic and English appear together, use Cairo and Outfit respectively
✗ Never Do
✗Never stretch, skew, or distort the logo
✗Never apply unauthorized colors or gradients to the logo
✗Never place the logo on a busy or low-contrast background without a clear container
✗Never use the logo at sizes below the minimum (24px / 8mm)
✗Never add drop shadows, glows, or 3D effects to the logo
✗Never outline or rasterize text — always use vector or web fonts
✗Never mix the dark and light backgrounds within the same layout section

Motion Principles

Kinetix is about movement. All animations reflect the agency's energy — fast, smooth, and purposeful.

Entrance Animations ease-out200–400ms
Elements enter from below with a fade. Use transform: translateY(16px) → translateY(0) combined with opacity: 0 → 1. Never use bounce or elastic effects on brand elements.
Hover States ease-in-out150ms
Buttons and interactive elements scale to 1.02 on hover with a subtle color shift toward lime. Links underline with a sliding animation from left to right.
Logo Animation cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)600ms
The K mark scales from 0.8 → 1.0 with a gradient sweep from left to right on page load. The wordmark follows 100ms after with a fade-in from right.
Page Transitions ease-in-out300ms
Cross-fade between pages with a subtle lime accent bar sweeping across the top at 2px height. Never block user interaction during transitions.
