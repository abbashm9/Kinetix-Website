✦ About Kinetix

The region exports oil.
We help it export brands.

Marketing · Technology · AI📍 Salmiya, Kuwait

Kinetix is a precision-led marketing, technology, and AI company in Kuwait, built on a simple conviction: the Middle East shouldn't just consume the world's brands and tools — it should build the brands and technology the world competes with. For too long the region has joined the global economy through oil alone. We're here to change that — combining world-class marketing with modern technology and AI to turn local companies into global competitors.

Experience the Kuwait market trusts

We've spent over eight years working in the Kuwait market, and in that time we've worked with 200+ companies — from young brands finding their feet to established businesses scaling up. That depth of local experience is what separates marketing that simply looks good from marketing that actually moves the numbers — here, in this market, with these customers.

Marketing, technology, and AI under one roof

We're not only a marketing team — we build the technology and AI that growth actually runs on. Websites and apps, automation, smart lead flows, custom AI tools, and data-driven systems sit alongside our brand and campaign work, so strategy and execution never get lost in handoffs between vendors.

We are deeply invested in artificial intelligence — not as a buzzword, but as both our edge and a service we deliver. We use AI to do more with a lean, senior team, and we build AI and automation for our clients. While most of the region is waiting to see what AI does, we're already using it to win — and helping our clients do the same.

Where we're going

Kinetix starts in marketing because that's the capability the region needs most. But the goal is larger: to become the partner businesses across the Gulf turn to for marketing, technology, and AI together — a regional firm built to global standards. We're early in that journey, and honest about it. But the direction is set.

What we believe

  • Precise — every dinar, every word, every pixel is accountable to a result.
  • Bilingual & global-minded — we build in Arabic and English, for Kuwait and beyond.
