# Flow brief — Kinetix website visuals

Paste the whole brief into Flow's agent. It plans the shots. The rules at the bottom are non-negotiable.

## The idea

Kinetix is an AI-first creative agency for businesses across the Gulf (Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, the GCC). The website reads like an Apple product page: bright, airy, precise, very clean, quietly techy. Its story: a business owner's day is full of small, real, annoying problems (unanswered messages, ads nobody can read, a brand that looks different on every post). Kinetix quietly handles them. By the end, the owner's day feels light.

The visuals are NOT about AI robots, glowing brains, circuit boards or holograms. They're about calm, competent, modern work in real Gulf business settings, shot like an Apple commercial.

## The look (same for every shot)

- Apple product-film style: soft daylight, white and warm-grey surfaces, shallow depth of field, slow deliberate camera, lots of empty space.
- Palette: near-white, warm grey, brushed aluminium, one restrained accent of the Kinetix lime (#C5E88A) appearing only as a small real object (a notebook edge, a cup, a phone case). Never as glow or light.
- Settings: modern Gulf business spaces. Riyadh or Jeddah style offices, boutique shopfronts, cafés, clinics. Architecture clean and contemporary. Nothing touristy, no desert clichés, no skyline postcards.
- People: optional, and only from behind, hands, or out of focus. No faces in focus. Modest, contemporary Gulf business dress where people appear.

## What the site needs (6 assets)

1. **Hero, left side — "Your day."** (still, 4:5 portrait, 2048px tall) A business owner's desk at 8am, slightly cluttered: phone face up with notifications, a coffee, an open laptop, printed papers. Calm but busy. Morning daylight.
2. **Hero, right side — "Handled."** (still, 4:5 portrait) The same desk, same angle, same light, but clear: closed laptop, one notebook, coffee, phone face down. Must match #1's framing exactly so the two sit side by side as a pair.
3. **Brand act texture** (still, 16:9) Close-up of premium printed brand material on a white table: business cards, a folded brochure, a paper bag, all blank (no logo, no text). We composite real client logos in code.
4. **Reporting act** (still, 16:9) Hands holding a tablet over a café table. The screen is plain white and blank (we put the real report UI on it in code). Soft daylight.
5. **PEAK clip — "Business in motion."** (VIDEO, 16:9, 8 seconds, and a 9:16 version of the same idea for mobile) One continuous, slow, forward dolly through a bright modern Gulf boutique or showroom opening for the day: lights coming on, a door opening, a customer silhouette entering, a phone on the counter lighting up with a message. The feeling is momentum starting. Steady camera, no cuts, no shake, constant speed (the scroll will scrub through it frame by frame, so even speed matters more than drama).
6. **Close** (still, 16:9) Golden-hour light across the same desk from #2, empty chair, the day done. Quiet, satisfied.

## Rules

- No text, letters, numbers, logos or UI anywhere in any frame. Screens are blank or off. We add every word in code.
- No AI clichés: no robots, holograms, glowing nodes, neural networks, blue tech light.
- No product or packaging of any real client.
- No faces in focus.
- No mention or visual of a specific country's flag or landmark.
- No audio needed on the clip (it gets stripped).
- #1 and #2 must be the same camera position. Generate them together, as a pair.
- Video: constant-speed camera, no cuts, no speed ramps, no handheld shake.
