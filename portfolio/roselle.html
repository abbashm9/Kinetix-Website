Case Study — Brand Identity

Roselle Loungewear

A Kuwaiti loungewear label with a bilingual identity — designed to sit comfortably beside the region's most polished retail brands.

ClientRoselle Loungewear
SectorFashion & Retail
LocationKuwait
ScopeBrand Identity
01 The Mark

A five-petal mark and a logotype that whispers.

Roselle's identity is built on softness: a five-petal floral mark, a refined serif logotype, and a bilingual system with a matching Arabic lockup — set in a palette of deep plum, dusty rose and warm cream.

02 Packaging

Unboxing as part of the product.

For loungewear, the moment the box opens is the first impression of comfort. The packaging system — bag, box, tags and labels — carries the identity into the customer's hands.

03 Retail Applications

Designed for the shop floor.

Storefront, signage and in-store presence — the identity was designed from day one to hold its own in Kuwait's most polished retail environments.

04 Digital-Ready

An identity that already speaks internet.

The system ships with everything the brand needs to go digital — social templates, an app icon, and an e-commerce design concept — so the identity translates to every screen without losing its softness.
