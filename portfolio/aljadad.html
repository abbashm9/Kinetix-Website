Case Study — Full Service Ongoing

Al-Jadad Center

A psychotherapy & couples-therapy center in Kuwait. Kinetix built the brand from the ground up — and stayed on as the center's marketing partner.

Brand Identity→Website→Social Media→Paid Ads
ClientAl-Jadad Center
SectorPsychotherapy & Couples Therapy
LocationKuwait
EngagementFull service, ongoing
01 Brand Identity

An identity built around calm.

A mental-health brand has to feel safe before it says a word. We built Al-Jadad's identity from the ground up: a flowing Arabic calligraphic logotype, a palette of soft olive greens and warm neutrals, and a bilingual system that carries the same quiet confidence in Arabic and English.

02 Website

A website that feels like the waiting room should.

We designed and developed aljadad.com end to end — an Arabic-first site where the calm of the identity carries straight through to the screen, and booking a session is never more than a tap away.

03 Social Media — Ongoing

Content that treats the reader gently.

Kinetix runs the center's social presence — a steady rhythm of educational content on topics like anxiety, depression and perfectionism, written with the center's clinicians in mind and designed entirely inside the brand system.

04 Paid Advertising — Ongoing

Reaching the people who are ready to reach out.

We plan and manage the center's paid campaigns — the same identity, the same tone of voice, put in front of the people searching for help. Creative, targeting and budgets are handled by the same team that built the brand, so nothing gets lost between strategy and spend.

From the first sketch of the logo to this month's campaigns — one partner across the whole journey.
