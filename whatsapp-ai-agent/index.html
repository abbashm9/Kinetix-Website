Questions people ask

What is a WhatsApp AI agent?

It is an AI assistant connected to your WhatsApp Business number. It reads incoming messages, answers from your own information, asks qualifying questions, books appointments and hands the conversation to a person when needed.

Does it understand Arabic and Gulf dialect?

Yes. It replies in Arabic or English depending on the customer, and we test it on Gulf dialect and mixed Arabic and English before launch.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. For an agent that runs reliably on a business number, it connects through the WhatsApp Business API via an approved provider. We set this up for you. The provider and Meta charge their own messaging fees.

How much does a WhatsApp AI agent cost?

Kinetix AI systems start from 750 KD, quoted as a fixed project after a short call. The quote covers the build, testing and handover.

Will it replace my team?

No. It takes the repetitive first replies off your team so they spend time on the conversations that need a person.
