How Much Does a Website Cost in Saudi Arabia?

By Kinetix TeamNovember 20265 min read

A website can cost almost anything, which makes it hard to budget. This article gives our price, explains what it covers, and says what makes it go up or down. For a Kuwait view, see how much a website costs in Kuwait.

The short answer

A business website at Kinetix starts from KWD 675 (about SAR 8,250). A website together with a brand identity starts from KWD 1,050 (about SAR 12,800). These are "from" prices, because the number of pages and features decides the final figure.

Prices are in Kuwaiti dinars. Riyal figures are approximate, using 1 KWD as roughly SAR 12.2.

What the website is built for

We build websites that are bilingual, so a customer in Riyadh can read Arabic and one abroad can read English. They are fast, because a slow page loses visitors before they read a word. And they are built to convert: clear pages whose job is to get a visitor to message you, call you or buy. More on the service: websites.

What moves the price up or down

  • Number of pages. A simple site with a few pages costs less than one with many.
  • Features. Forms, bookings or a shop add work, and each one changes the number.
  • Languages. Arabic needs right-to-left layout done properly, not just translated text.
  • Content. If the text and images are ready, the build is quicker. If we write and source them, it takes longer.

With a brand identity

If you do not yet have a logo, colours and fonts you are happy with, do the identity first or together. Identity plus website starts from KWD 1,050. The site is then designed from the identity from day one. See what a brand identity costs in the GCC.

How to choose what fits

If you have a clear brand and need to sell online or collect enquiries, a website alone is enough. If you are starting out or your brand looks different everywhere, take identity and website together. Not sure? Tell us what the site must do for you, and we will say what it needs.
